GPSinfo is a focused, offline-first GPS dashboard and guided-run companion for Android.

Every metric your phone's GPS chip can report — laid out on a single scrollable screen, with optional GPX trail recording, Bluetooth heart-rate, and a Sports view that coaches your pace without phoning home. No Google Maps SDK, no Play Services, no telemetry, no accounts, no in-app purchases.

Built for hikers, sailors, cyclists, runners, motorcyclists, ham operators, GNSS-curious tinkerers, and anyone who wants their location data to stay on their phone.

▌ DASHBOARD
• Position in five formats — DMS, decimal, Plus Code, Maidenhead grid, MGRS. Tap to cycle, long-press to copy.
• Altitude, per-axis accuracy and a live fix-quality badge
• Satellite count and signal strength across GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou, QZSS, SBAS and IRNSS
• Sky view with constellation-coloured satellites at their real azimuth and elevation
• Tilt-corrected compass with magnetic + true heading, declination and inclination
• Dedicated magnetometer calibration screen with hard-iron offset estimate
• Speed gauge with auto-adapting scale and a HUD-mirror mode for windshield reflections
• Sun position, sunrise / sunset, day length, night-side terminator on the world map

▌ TRAILS
• Record GPS trails as standard GPX 1.1 with Garmin TrackPointExtension (speed, course, heart rate)
• Foreground service keeps recording while the app is backgrounded or the screen is off
• Render the trail on OpenStreetMap with polyline and tap-to-inspect markers
• Speed, elevation and heart-rate charts; scrubber to inspect any trackpoint
• Heading-up map mode, sun arc overlay
• Pre-cache map tiles for offline use
• Simplify with Ramer–Douglas–Peucker to clean GPS noise
• Import, share or save GPX anywhere; one-tap waypoint capture

▌ NAVIGATION
• Track-back: relative-bearing arrow walks you back along any recorded trail
• Tap-on-map destination picker for ad-hoc bearing-to-waypoint runs
• Live distance, ETA and pace-vs-target read-out per waypoint

▌ SPORTS VIEW (guided runs & walks)
• Full-screen dashboard: elapsed + distance, live and average pace, speed, cadence and stride (from the device pedometer)
• Pace-deviation gauge — too fast / on target / too slow at a glance
• Heart-rate zone gauge with configurable zones (and optional per-trail override)
• Intensity profile of the next stretch, coloured by grade, with ETA to the next climb
• Pace targets — overall or per-segment along a route
• Optional audible coaching cues via on-device text-to-speech; the navigation-guidance audio attribute ducks your music instead of cutting it
• Post-run performance score (0–100) blending pace adherence with HR-zone adherence

▌ BLUETOOTH HEART-RATE
• Pair any standard BLE heart-rate monitor (Polar, Wahoo, Garmin chest belts, …)
• Direct GATT connection — no vendor SDKs, no cloud bridges, no API keys
• Live BPM card on the dashboard; samples persisted per trackpoint in GPX

▌ PRIVACY
• No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or third-party tracker
• Location data, heart-rate samples and TTS audio all stay on your device
• The only network call the app ever makes is anonymous OpenStreetMap tile fetches, and only when you open a trail map
• Full policy: https://jeroentrappers.github.io/gpsinfo/privacy.html

▌ NO PLAY SERVICES, NO PLAY BILLING
Runs on de-Googled devices (GrapheneOS, LineageOS, /e/OS, …). Location comes straight from the Android GNSS APIs. No in-app purchase — donations live on Liberapay and GitHub Sponsors, never gated behind a feature.

▌ TWELVE LANGUAGES
English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (BR), Russian, Japanese, Czech, Turkish, Polish — each native-speaker reviewed.
