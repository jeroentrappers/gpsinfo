GPSinfo is a focused, offline-first GPS dashboard for Android.

Everything you'd want to see at once — your position, satellite signals, compass, speed, altitude, sun position — laid out on a single scrollable dashboard. No Google Maps SDK, no Play Services, no telemetry, no accounts.

Built for hikers, sailors, cyclists, motorcyclists, GNSS-curious tinkerers, and anyone who wants their location data to stay on their phone.

▌ DASHBOARD
• Position in WGS84 (DMS or decimal), with altitude and per-axis accuracy
• Live satellite count and signal strength across GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou, QZSS, SBAS and IRNSS
• Sky view with constellation-coloured satellites at their real azimuth and elevation
• Tilt-corrected compass with magnetic + true heading, declination and inclination
• Speed gauge with auto-adapting scale and a HUD-mirror mode for windshield reflections
• Sun position, sunrise / sunset, day length, night-side terminator on the world map

▌ TRAILS
• Record GPS trails as standard GPX 1.1 with Garmin TrackPointExtension (speed, course)
• Foreground service keeps recording while the app is backgrounded or the screen is off
• Render the trail on OpenStreetMap with a polyline plus tap-to-inspect markers
• Speed + elevation chart and a scrubber to inspect any single trackpoint
• Heading-up map mode, sun arc overlay
• Pre-cache map tiles for an area so the trail map works fully offline
• Simplify trails with Ramer–Douglas–Peucker to clean GPS noise
• Import GPX from anywhere; share or save as GPX anywhere
• One-tap waypoint capture

▌ PRIVACY
• No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no third-party tracker.
• Location data never leaves your device.
• The only network call the app ever makes is anonymous OpenStreetMap tile fetches, and only when you open a trail map.
• Full privacy policy: https://jeroentrappers.github.io/gpsinfo/privacy.html

▌ NO PLAY SERVICES
This app does not depend on Google Play Services and runs on de-Googled devices (GrapheneOS, LineageOS, /e/OS, …). Location comes straight from the Android GNSS APIs.
