What it does

Everything the GPS chip can tell you — and a guided-run coach.

Raw GNSS, 7 constellations

Per-satellite signal strength and sky position across GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou, QZSS, SBAS and IRNSS — straight from the Android GNSS APIs.

Five coordinate formats

DMS, Decimal, Plus Code, Maidenhead grid, MGRS. Tap to cycle; long-press to copy. Ham radio, SAR and geocaching audiences welcome.

Trail recording with HR

Standard GPX 1.1 with Garmin gpxtpx extensions for speed, course and per-point heart rate. Foreground-service backed; survives screen-off and app-backgrounding.

Offline trail map

OpenStreetMap-backed map with on-demand tile pre-cache for a region, so the trail map works fully offline once you've prepared an area.

Bluetooth heart-rate

Pair a standard BLE heart-rate monitor (Polar, Wahoo, Garmin chest belts). Configurable zones, live BPM card on the dashboard, per-point HR persisted in GPX.

Sports Dashboard

Full-screen guided-run view: elapsed + distance header, live-vs-avg pace / speed / cadence / stride, pace- deviation gauge, HR-zone gauge, intensity profile of the next stretch coloured by grade, ETA to next climb.

Pace targets & coaching cues

Set an overall pace target or per-segment targets along a route. On-device TextToSpeech announces "Too fast", "Too slow", zone changes — ducks instead of cutting your music. Off by default.

Performance scoring

Completed trails with a target pace get a 0–100 score badge — pace adherence plus HR-zone adherence when a monitor was connected. Pure local computation, no cloud.

Track-back navigation

Start track-back from any recorded trail and a relative- bearing arrow walks you back to where you started, or anywhere via a tap-on-map destination picker.

Tilt-corrected compass

Magnetic + true heading, declination, inclination, plus a magnetometer-calibration screen with hard-iron offset estimate and orientation-coverage feedback.

12 languages

English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (BR), Russian, Japanese, Czech, Turkish, Polish. Each native-speaker reviewed.

No Play Services

Runs on de-Googled devices (GrapheneOS, LineageOS, /e/OS, …). Location comes straight from LocationManager + GnssStatus. No Play Billing either — donations live on Liberapay and GitHub Sponsors.

Privacy

The default is silence.

Most GPS apps phone home before they show you a coordinate. GPSinfo is the opposite — the only network call it ever makes is an anonymous OpenStreetMap tile fetch, and only when you actively open a trail map.

  • No analytics SDK
  • No crash reporter
  • No ads, no attribution SDK
  • No Firebase, no Google Analytics
  • No social SDKs, no Facebook pixel
  • No account, ever
  • Doesn't read your contacts, photos, or other apps' data
  • Doesn't send your location anywhere
